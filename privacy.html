Sirra Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you use the Sirra mobile app ("Sirra", "the app"), why it is processed, and what your rights are. Data controller: Tekfors Elektronik Otomasyon San. Tic. Ltd. Şti., Ahi Evran Mah. ASO Bulvarı, Sinova İş Merkezi No: 4/65, Sincan, Ankara, Türkiye. Contact: sirra.app@gmail.com.

Sirra is an anonymous dating and chat app. Your real name, email address and account ID are never shown to other users.

1. Data we collect

Account information

  • The account ID and email address received when you sign in with Google or Apple (used only for sign-in and account recovery; never shown to anyone). Apple may let you hide your email address.

Profile information (visible to other users)

  • Nickname, avatar, profile photos, status line, about text, gender, the gender you are interested in, what you are looking for, height (optional), age (calculated from your birth year; you can hide it), city, country and verification badge.

Location

  • If you allow it, your approximate location. Your location is rounded on your phone to roughly 1 km, and only this rounded value is stored on our servers. Other users never see your location — only your city and your distance in whole kilometres. Location is used to show distances, list people near you and power the "Crossed paths" feature. You can use the app without granting location access.

Usage data

  • Likes, passes, matches, last active time, online status, app language and time zone, daily login streak.

Messages and media

  • Your chat messages and the photos, videos and voice messages you send. Media sent as "view once" becomes inaccessible after the recipient opens it and is deleted from the server. When you clear a chat or unsend a message, the content is deleted.
  • Screenshots are blocked on chat screens.

Verification

  • The selfie you submit for the blue check is used only by our authorized team to compare against your profile and is never shown to other users.

Device information

  • Your device's push notification token, so we can send you notifications.

Payments

  • Purchases are made through Google Play or the App Store. We never receive your card details; we only receive a purchase record (product, date, status).

2. Why we process your data

  • To create your account and present you in the app (performance of a contract).
  • To run features such as matching, chat, games and vibration (performance of a contract).
  • Safety and community rules (legitimate interest and legal obligation):
  • Messages are automatically scanned for redirection to external apps, sharing of contact details, scams and language suggesting minors. Scanning is done on our servers using rules and a small model we trained ourselves; messages are not sent to any third party for this purpose.
  • If something looks suspicious, the person's chats may be paused temporarily and our authorized team may review the relevant chats. Accounts are never closed automatically.
  • Photos are checked for nudity on your phone before being sent; the photo is not sent anywhere for this check.
  • Reports and blocks are reviewed.
  • Message and game translation: when you use translation, the relevant text is sent to our translation provider (DeepL); the result is stored on our servers so it does not need to be translated again.
  • To send notifications: new messages, matches, likes and occasional tips or reminders. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • To improve the service and fix errors.

3. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers that are needed to run the service, and only as much as necessary:

  • Supabase — database, file storage and authentication (servers in Frankfurt, Germany — European Union).
  • Google / Apple — sign-in and in-app purchases.
  • Expo and Google Firebase — push notifications.
  • DeepL — translation (only the text being translated).
  • RevenueCat — purchase and subscription management (once enabled).
  • Competent public authorities, where required by law.

Information you make public on your profile and information you voluntarily give to other users in chats (e.g. phone number, social media account, photos) ends up in those users' hands. We have no control over how other users use such information and are not responsible for it.

Some of these providers are located abroad, so your data may be transferred outside your country. Such transfers are made in line with the safeguards required by KVKK and the GDPR.

4. How long we keep data

  • Your profile and chat data are kept for as long as your account is active.
  • When someone leaves a chat, its messages are kept for at most 7 days so that possible reports can be reviewed, and are then deleted.
  • View-once media is deleted after it is opened.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, photos and content belonging to you are deleted. Records needed for legal obligations (e.g. purchase records, review records) may be kept for the legally required period.

5. Your rights

Under KVKK (Turkish Law No. 6698) and the GDPR, you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, to request information, to have your data corrected or deleted, to object to processing, to data portability and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. You can send requests to sirra.app@gmail.com. You can delete your account at any time via Profile → Delete account in the app.

6. Age limit

Sirra is only for people aged 18 and over. We close accounts that we find belong to anyone under 18.

7. Security

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected in our database by access rules. No system is completely secure; in the event of a breach, we will notify you and the relevant authorities within the legally required time.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. We will notify you in the app of significant changes.
